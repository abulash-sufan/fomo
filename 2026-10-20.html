Rubio says India-US trade deal very close after Mumbai meet

  • What happened: US Secretary of State Marco Rubio met External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar in Mumbai on Tuesday to discuss trade, energy security and defence cooperation.
  • Key details: Rubio told reporters the trade deal is 'very, very close' with only a small number of items left to wrap up, signalling progress after months of stalled negotiations.
  • Background: Washington imposed up to 50% tariffs on Indian goods in August 2025; a February 2026 framework reduced this to 18%, but talks later plateaued according to Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman.
  • Energy issues: Discussions covered energy cooperation despite differences over India's continued Russian crude purchases; New Delhi says it will expand US imports while diversifying suppliers.
  • Trilateral meet: Rubio, Jaishankar and UAE Foreign Minister Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed Al Nahyan also held a joint meeting in Mumbai on Tuesday.
  • What's next: Rubio departs Wednesday for Jamnagar to visit the Reliance energy refinery and Vantara animal facility, hosted by the Ambani family as a private visit.

JNU students end 17-day hunger strike after admin assurances

  • What happened: JNUSU members ended their indefinite hunger strike on Tuesday after meetings with a university committee headed by Dean Vivek Kumar.
  • Key demands met: The committee directed revocation of rustication orders against five students, including JNUSU president Aditi Mishra, vice-president Gopika and general secretary Sunil.
  • Committee decisions: All FIRs against students will be withdrawn; committees with JNUSU representation will review the CPO Manual, JNUEE processes and hostel reservations.
  • Proctorial inquiries: Proctorial enquiries initiated during earlier protests against rustication, UGC regulations and hostel infrastructure will be withdrawn after verification.
  • Political intervention: CPI(M) MP John Brittas had urged President Murmu on Monday to intervene under Section 8 of the JNU Act, citing deteriorating health of strikers on day 17.
  • VC stance: Vice-Chancellor Santishree Pandit claimed a competent authority offered to meet students on October 16 but none were present; students disputed this narrative.
