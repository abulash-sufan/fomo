  • IOC Cuts West Asia Oil

    Indian Oil Corp plans to slightly reduce its West Asian crude purchases under annual deals in 2027/28 due to supply uncertainties caused by the ongoing Iran war. IOC had initially planned to source 47% of its crude imports from West Asia under annual free-on-board deals in the financial year ending March 2027. Disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz have driven freight rates to near-record highs, prompting refiners to shift to spot purchases and increase Russian oil imports. IOC paused plans to acquire two very large gas carriers after costs nearly doubled to $150 million for a 10-year-old vessel since the tender was issued in July.

Nigeria confirms first Ebola case linked to East Africa outbreak

  • What happened: Nigeria confirmed an Ebola case in a female patient who travelled from Uganda to Abuja. She developed symptoms on 7 October and was admitted to a private hospital in Abuja on 11 October. She is in isolation and receiving medical care.
  • Key details: The patient is not Nigerian and is the wife of a diplomat. Health authorities have identified 44 close contacts and are tracing healthcare workers, patients, caregivers and travel contacts. The Bundibugyo strain has no approved vaccine or treatment.
  • Reactions: Nigeria's Health Minister Muhammad Ali Pate said they are doing everything possible to contain the case. Africa CDC called for urgent international financing, medical supplies, diagnostics and trained responders, calling it the most geographically widespread Ebola outbreak in history.
  • What's next: Contact-tracing continues using her travel documents. Uganda's health ministry said there are no confirmed active Ebola cases in Uganda and urged the public to remain calm and practise handwashing.
