BJP and Akali Dal revive alliance for 2027 Punjab polls

  • Announcement: Amit Shah, BJP president Nitin Nabin and SAD chief Sukhbir Singh Badal formally announced the alliance at a joint press conference at BJP headquarters in Delhi on Tuesday.
  • Key details: The parties will jointly contest all 117 assembly seats with the Akali Dal in the lead; Shah said tackling drugs, corruption and law and order are the alliance's key priorities.
  • Background: The long-standing alliance broke in September 2020 when SAD quit the NDA over the three farm laws, which were later repealed; the two parties had governed Punjab together for 15 years between 1997 and 2017.
  • Recent performance: In the 2022 elections contested separately, SAD won three seats and BJP won two, while AAP swept the state.
  • Reaction: Shah declared 'Bada bhai will be CM', indicating the Akali Dal will hold the chief minister's post if the alliance wins.
  • What's next: Punjab BJP announced a 16-member state election committee headed by Kewal Singh Dhillon to prepare the electoral roadmap and a chargesheet against the incumbent government.
