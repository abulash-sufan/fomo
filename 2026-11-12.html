Retail inflation hits 2-year high of 6.09% in October

  • What happened: India's retail inflation, measured by CPI, surged to a two-year high of 6.09% in October, breaching the RBI's upper tolerance limit of 6% for the first time since October 2024.
  • Numbers: Food inflation jumped to 8.94% from 7.74% in September; rural inflation stood at 6.63% and urban at 5.41%; Tamil Nadu recorded the highest state inflation at 7.29%.
  • Background: Inflation has risen for 12 straight months since bottoming at 0.04% in October 2025; the RBI raised the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.5% in October and signalled further hikes.
  • Reaction: Economists Madan Sabnavis and Radhika Rao expect the RBI to hike rates by 25-50 bps at the December 2-4 MPC meeting, citing persistent food price pressures and broadening core inflation.
  • What's next: The October data will be a key input for the RBI's December monetary policy review; the ongoing harvest and kharif output will determine the trajectory of food prices in coming months.
