CapitaLand opens 53 MW Chennai data centre targeting hyperscalers

  • What happened: CapitaLand Investment opened its data centre in Chennai, its second in India, with 53 MW capacity after completion by end 2026, inaugurated by Tamil Nadu CM Vijay and Singapore's Deputy PM Gan Kim Yong.

  • TN govt rice sale

    Tamil Nadu food and civil supplies department will procure 25,000 tonnes of fine variety rice and sell it through cooperative and Amutham stores at ₹30 per kg, starting this weekend. Up to 5 kg will be sold per person; the govt will also sell toor dal at ₹130/kg, parboiled rice at ₹60/kg, urad dal at ₹140/kg, garlic at ₹240/kg and red chilli at ₹330/kg via Amutham stores.
