FIR filed against comedian Yash Maheshwari over Chhath remarks

  • What happened: Patna Cyber Police registered an FIR on November 24 against stand-up comedian Yash Maheshwari, known as @Almostyash, for allegedly making derogatory and sexually explicit remarks about Bihar women applying sindoor during Chhath Puja in a YouTube video titled 'Morally Questionable' uploaded on November 21.
  • Legal sections: The case was filed under BNS Sections 75(1) (sexual harassment), 79 (insulting modesty of a woman), 351 (criminal intimidation), 352 (intentional insult to provoke breach of peace) and IT Act Sections 66C and 66D.
  • Police statement: Patna Police stated the remarks could hurt women's dignity, disturb social harmony, create law-and-order problems and hurt public sentiments on a wider scale.
  • Political reaction: Congress leader Abhishek Singhvi called the remarks a 'vile assault' on cultural and women's dignity, while BJP activist Ritu Jaiswal demanded FIRs in every Bihar district and strict legal action.
  • Commission action: The Bihar State Women's Commission took suo motu cognisance, writing to SSP Kartikeya K Sharma seeking immediate FIR, early arrest, removal of the video clip and an action-taken report.
  • Apology: Maheshwari released a public apology video on November 24 admitting his remarks about Bihar's cultural traditions were inappropriate, accepting responsibility and promising to be more careful with future content.
  • What's next: Patna Police said the investigation is underway and further action will be taken in accordance with the law, while public demands for legal action continue despite the apology.

SC to hear contempt plea over unlit Karthigai Deepam

  • What happened: The Supreme Court agreed to hear a contempt plea against the Tamil Nadu government for not lighting the traditional Karthigai Deepam lamp at Deepathoon atop Thiruparankundram hill on November 24, despite a November 21 order permitting it as a one-time measure.
  • Key details: State counsel informed the bench of Justices Aravind Kumar and Vipul M Pancholi that the lamp had not been lit and a status report was being filed; the petitioner's counsel alleged deliberate non-compliance and that the government had assured the Madras High Court it would comply until the last moment.
  • Reaction: BJP MP Sudhanshu Trivedi accused the state government and the INDIA bloc of the 'greatest act of contempt', alleging the refusal insulted Hindu sentiments and was motivated by a desire to 'eradicate Sanatan Dharma' driven by an international conspiracy.
  • What's next: The Supreme Court bench told the petitioner to file the contempt petition formally, stating 'File it, and then we will see,' indicating the matter will proceed on the contempt plea.
