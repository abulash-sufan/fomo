  • Starlink battlefield importance

    Starlink satellites operated by SpaceX provide high-speed internet vital for Ukraine's battlefield communications and effective drone warfare. Using Starlink, Ukrainian drones can stay connected to operators over longer distances than conventional radio networks and are less vulnerable to jamming. On Tuesday night, Starlink restricted drone speeds above 100mph (160km/h), causing loss of connection and disrupting missions. A top Ukrainian official said the restriction disrupted battlefield missions and hampered Kyiv's ability to protect itself from incoming Russian attacks. Connectivity was restored by Wednesday evening. Starlink is the world's largest satellite constellation with over 10,000 active satellites. Starshield is its military-grade sister service designed for the Pentagon with enhanced encryption. The US government has allowed Ukraine to use both services in its defence against Russia.
