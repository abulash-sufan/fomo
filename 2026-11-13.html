  • What happened: A Range Rover driven by Raghavendra Kushwaha collided with a Rapido bike-taxi near Shilaj, Ahmedabad, around 6:45 am on Wednesday, killing passenger Harshadkumar Purbia and injuring driver Pradeep Kumar Darwal.
