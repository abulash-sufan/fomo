17 Convicts Escape Theni Jail via Epilepsy Ruse

  • What Happened: At around 11 pm on Monday, 17 undertrials escaped from Thekkampatti sub-jail in Theni district by staging a drama that one was having epileptic fits. When a warder entered the cell, inmates assaulted him and fled.
  • Key Details: One convict was recaptured near the jail within hours. By Tuesday 5 pm, 13 of the 17 had been rearrested. The injured guard was identified as Gr II constable Thangapandi, admitted to Theni Government Medical College Hospital.
  • Reactions: Opposition parties targeted the ruling TVK, with AIADMK leader Edappadi K Palaniswami calling it a humiliating security failure. MK Stalin questioned how such breaches occur despite modern surveillance.
  • What's Next: 17 special teams are combing forest areas and bordering districts including Madurai, Dindigul, and Idukki in Kerala. Police have set up 16 check-posts and shared photos of escapees with neighboring states.

Jordan strikes Syria after church attack, civilians killed

  • What happened: Jordanian fighter jets struck a village in Syria's Daraa province on Monday evening, targeting what Amman called terrorist organisations in retaliation for Friday's church attack in Karak.
  • Casualties: Syrian health officials and state media reported five to seven civilians killed, including women, children, and an elderly person, while Jordan claimed it killed and wounded several terrorists.
  • Church attack: Nine people were killed and 34 injured in a shooting at a Greek Orthodox funeral in Karak on Friday, prompting Jordan's retaliatory action.
  • Arrests: Jordanian security forces arrested two brothers and their brother-in-law, all Jordanians, found hiding in an abandoned house in Karak before dawn on Monday.
  • Coordination dispute: Jordan said it coordinated with the Syrian government, but Damascus denied being notified and condemned the strikes as an unacceptable breach of airspace.
  • Border closure: Syria closed the Nassib border crossing, the only passenger and commercial crossing between the two countries, suspending all traffic in both directions.
  • What's next: Syria opened direct communication channels with Jordan and launched comprehensive investigations into the targeting, while the Kremlin suggested the truce might set a precedent for future localised ceasefires.
