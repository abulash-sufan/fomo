Tamil Nadu

CM Vijay felicitates toppers, urges SC bench in Chennai

  • Felicitation Event: Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay felicitated Class 10 and 12 toppers from 30 Assembly constituencies across five districts at a Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam function in Thiruvanmiyur, Chennai.
  • Advice to Students: Vijay urged students to nurture individuality, avoid bribery, corruption, and drugs, stating he attended as an elder brother rather than CM and that they should focus on studies, not politics or 2029 elections.
  • Overcoming Obstacles: He told students that obstacles signal others recognise their potential for success and urged them to stay strong and steadfast with conviction and confidence.
  • SC Bench Demand: In a letter to Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Vijay urged the Centre to pursue a permanent Supreme Court bench in Chennai under Article 130, citing difficulties for southern litigants accessing justice in Delhi.
  • Historical Context: The proposal was first mooted by Tamil Nadu in 1980, rejected by the Supreme Court in 2007 despite infrastructure assurances, and supported by multiple parliamentary committees and Law Commission reports since 2004.
  • Justification: Vijay argued Chennai has the legal expertise and infrastructure, noting the Madras High Court's 1862 establishment, and asserted a regional bench would not affect judicial independence as control remains with the CJI.
