Chinese researchers warn of rising flood risks from Brahmaputra dam project

  • Findings: Chinese researchers from the Chinese Academy of Sciences and Yunnan University reconstructed the Brahmaputra's runoff history from 1325 to 2020 using tree-ring data, projecting increased runoff and flood risks this century.
  • Project Details: The $168 billion mega dam project in Tibet's Medog region will have over 60,000 MW capacity, making it nearly three times larger than China's Three Gorges Dam.
  • Concerns: Researchers warned in July that an active fault line beneath the planned dam could threaten structural stability, urging engineers to strengthen slopes against landslides.
  • Regional Impact: The river flows into India as the Siang in Arunachal Pradesh before becoming the Brahmaputra, crucial for agriculture and ecosystems across northeastern India and Bangladesh.
