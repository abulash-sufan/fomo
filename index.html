Tamil Nadu CM Vijay suspends Theni jail superintendent over 17 inmate escape

  • The incident: On October 26, 2026, 17 inmates at Theni District Jail escaped after attacking a guard during a fake medical emergency, using stolen keys to flee into forested areas.
  • The escape: All 17 escapees were apprehended within 40 hours through a massive search operation involving 17 police teams, five special task forces, four battalions, and drones scanning dense forests.
  • The suspension: Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay suspended Deputy Jail Superintendent N. Nageswaran with effect from October 30, citing negligence in supervision and security, under the Tamil Nadu Civil Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules.
  • The transfers: Superintendent of Madurai Central Prison R. Krishnaraj was transferred to Salem Central Prison, while DGP (Prisons) Sandeep Rai Rathore was moved to TANGEDCO as chief vigilance officer and K Shankar appointed new ADGP for Prisons.
  • The response: The government ordered stringent security measures, CCTV surveillance upgrades, and disciplinary action against negligent officials, with minister R Nirmal Kumar acknowledging procedural lapses.
