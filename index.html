Amit Shah assures Siang Valley residents of dialogue on mega dam project

  • What happened: Union Home Minister Amit Shah addressed concerns over the 11,200-MW Siang Upper Multipurpose Project during a visit to Itanagar on November 21, 2026, stating the project is of national importance and strategic value against China's upstream plans.
  • Key details: Shah promised one of the country's most attractive rehabilitation packages and assured that local culture, traditions, and religion would not be compromised, urging opponents to engage in dialogue with the state government.
  • Background: Indigenous Adi communities in Siang and Upper Siang districts have opposed the dam over fears of displacement, submergence of ancestral lands, and ecological damage in the fragile, seismically active valley.
  • Reaction: Protests turned violent in October with demonstrators torching a PSU office, a police station, and vandalising residences of three political leaders; four people were injured in police tear gas shelling.
  • What's next: Adi Ba:né Kébang United and Siang Indigenous Farmers' Forum submitted a memorandum demanding an immediate halt to pre-feasibility activities, release of detained protesters, withdrawal of security forces, and a judicial inquiry into the October 23 violence.

Cyclone Akvan weakens but brings heavy rain alerts for Odisha and West Bengal

  • What happened: Cyclone Akvan made landfall near Gopalpur in Odisha's Ganjam district on Friday morning with winds of 70-80 kmph gusting to 90 kmph, weakening into a deep depression by 11:30 am and further into a low-pressure area by Saturday.
  • Key details: IMD issued a red warning for Mayurbhanj and Balasore districts in Odisha, orange for 14 others, and yellow for the rest; West Bengal districts including North and South 24 Parganas, East and West Midnapore, Jhargram, Bankura, and Howrah face heavy to very heavy rainfall (7-20 cm).
  • Numbers: Digha recorded 176 mm rainfall in 24 hours till 8:30 am Saturday, Kanthi 105 mm, Sagar Island 85 mm, and Kolkata 41 mm; 26,173 people relocated to 145 relief camps in Bhadrak district.
  • Impact: Continuous heavy rain made the situation critical in Mayurbhanj with ODRAF and fire services deployed at three locations; rail services cancelled or rescheduled, flights affected at Bhubaneswar, Kolkata, and Visakhapatnam.
  • What's next: IMD forecasts the system to move northwards over Odisha, weakening further; heavy rain warnings extend to Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Bihar, and parts of the Northeast for November 21-22; fishermen advised not to venture into sea for 12 hours.

Drones hit Russian cargo ship in Gulf of Finland; crew evacuated

  • The attack: Two drones struck the Sparta II, a Russian cargo ship bound for Kaliningrad, west of Gogland Island on Saturday morning, about three nautical miles from Russia's border, per Leningrad governor Alexander Drozdenko.
  • Ship on fire: The vessel caught fire and drifted into Finnish territorial waters off Loviisa. A Border Guard patrol plane photographed flames and thick smoke at the ship's rear; Finland's Border Guard says the ship has known links to the Russian armed forces.
  • Rescue effort: Finnish Border Guard units were dispatched and said they were preparing to evacuate people and help fight the fire. The crew had not been forced to abandon ship and no environmental damage was reported so far.
  • Restrictions: Finland temporarily imposed air and sea traffic restrictions in the eastern Gulf of Finland as a precaution in case more drones needed intercepting, and said it had notified its allies.
  • Reaction: Finnish PM Petteri Orpo wrote on X that "war is spilling over into our immediate vicinity" and said Ukraine has the right to fight back against the aggressor. It is not clear who launched the attack.
  • Damage unclear: Drozdenko said the extent of damage had yet to be fully assessed. Kaliningrad governor Alexey Besprozvannykh said supplies to the Russian exclave were not at risk.
  • Background: Earlier this month Finland said it detected a strike on a Russian warship in the gulf, which Ukraine claimed, and Helsinki raised concerns with Kyiv. The gulf lies between Finland and Estonia, both NATO members.

Police hold Ganapathi homam after 40-day search for missing girl

  • What happened: Police performed a Ganapathi homam at Sathiyamangalam police station in Villupuram district while searching for seven-year-old Hemalatha, missing since October 12 from Nadupattu village near Alampoondi.
  • Key details: Her mother reported her missing after she disappeared while sleeping in the early hours. Police searched nearby areas including a forest and brick kiln under inspector Suresh Babu and Villupuram SP Mathivanan.
  • Case transfer: The case was transferred to CB-CID on October 21 after initial searches found no clues. A special CB-CID team continued the investigation.
  • Skeletal remains: Police found skeletal remains in a forest area, but tests showed they belonged to a man aged around 50, not the missing girl.
  • Reactions: Inspector Suresh Babu and other personnel participated in the homam inside the police station. A video of the ritual circulated on social media on Saturday.
  • What's next: Police have not disclosed any breakthrough in the case.
  • Political row: NDTV reported the station's sub-inspector recently met with an accident and other untoward incidents were reported within station limits over the past month.
