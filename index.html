Search intensified for five missing Kasimedu fishermen after cyclone

  • What happened: Nine fishermen from Kasimedu left Chennai on October 29 and faced engine failure near Ramayapatnam Port, Andhra Pradesh, on November 3 during Cyclone Murasu.
  • Numbers: Four bodies were recovered by the Indian Coast Guard on November 5 along the Prakasam district coast and at sea; five fishermen remain missing.
  • Government action: Finance Minister N. Marie Wilson is camping in Andhra Pradesh on Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay's orders to coordinate the search and transport of bodies from RIMS Hospital, Ongole.
  • Compensation: The state sanctioned an initial relief of ₹7 lakh per deceased family and will bear full costs for transporting bodies back to Kasimedu.
  • Reaction: DMK MP Kanimozhi and Leader of Opposition Udhayanidhi Stalin criticised the TVK government for negligence, delayed rescue, and demanded higher compensation than ₹7 lakh.
  • Safety lapses: Minister Wilson stated the boat lacked mandatory safety gear, commercial insurance, and regulatory documents, promising stricter enforcement of marine safety protocols.
  • Protest: Grieving family members and women from the Kasimedu fishing community confronted Minister Wilson over alleged administrative delays before his departure to Andhra Pradesh.
