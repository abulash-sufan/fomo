US warns Iran plots against Americans in advanced stages

  • What happened: US Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton sent an unclassified memo dated October 23 to Secretary of State Marco Rubio warning that Iranian lethal plots against US citizens have reached advanced stages in the Middle East and other countries.
  • Key details: The memo states Iran has substantially increased efforts to target US personnel overseas; CIA Director John Ratcliffe was copied, and officials say some intelligence indicates Iranian operatives are already active inside the US.
  • Diplomatic drawdown: The State Department ordered non-essential staff and families to leave Saudi Arabia and placed embassies in Jordan, Lebanon, Qatar, and the UAE on restricted operations, though staff there are not required to depart.
  • Houthi factor: Escalating Houthi missile and drone attacks on Saudi infrastructure, including oil facilities and airports, are cited alongside Iranian plots as drivers for the staffing reductions.
  • Military context: The alert comes amid reported US and Israeli plans for a new military campaign against Iran following US mid-term elections next month; Trump said Friday the US may do another 'little epic fury' referencing the February operation.
  • Reaction: A US official played down Iran's capacity to execute attacks inside the US, saying it lacks the ability to follow through on all its ambitions, while the NYT cited an official confirming the memo's authenticity on behalf of the Trump administration.
  • What's next: The intelligence community is working to downgrade sensitive information for wider sharing with State Department personnel as diplomatic efforts to return to formal negotiations with Tehran face uncertainty.

IMD warns of cyclone brewing in Bay of Bengal

  • What happened: The IMD forecast extremely heavy rainfall across south peninsular India following the onset of the Northeast monsoon over Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Karaikal, Coastal Andhra Pradesh, Rayalaseema, South Interior Karnataka, and Kerala and Mahe.
  • What happened: A low-pressure area over the south Bay of Bengal is expected to intensify into a cyclonic storm by October 26 and move west-northwestward toward the north Andhra Pradesh–south Odisha coasts.
  • What happened: The IMD issued a red alert for Nilgiris district (expected rainfall exceeding 204.4 mm) and orange alerts for Coimbatore, Tiruvannamalai, Villupuram, Kallakurichi, Cuddalore, Mayiladuthurai, Thanjavur, Tiruvarur, Nagapattinam, Puducherry, and Karaikal (115.6–204.4 mm).
  • Reaction: Fisherfolk were advised not to venture into the sea from October 26. District administrations were asked to take precautionary measures against waterlogging and landslides.
  • What's next: The next cyclonic storm in the North Indian Ocean will be named Arnab (Bangladesh contribution). A previous system crossed the Andhra Pradesh–Odisha coast as a deep depression on September 23.
