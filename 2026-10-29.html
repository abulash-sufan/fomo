Tamil Nadu Prisons DGP transferred days after Theni jailbreak

  • What happened: On Monday (October 26) night, 17 remand prisoners escaped from Thekkampatti sub-jail in Theni district after claiming an inmate suffered epileptic seizures and overpowering warders.
  • Recapture: Special teams nabbed 13 on Tuesday (October 27) and the remaining four on Wednesday (October 28).
  • Reactions: On October 27, nine guards were suspended and DGP Sandeep Rai Rathore visited Theni prison, saying human error led to the escape. A detailed inquiry was ordered.
  • DGP transfer: On Thursday (October 29), the Tamil Nadu government transferred DGP Sandeep Rai Rathore to DGP, EB, Vigilance, Chennai. K. Shankar, ADGP Enforcement Bureau CID, replaced him as ADGP, Prisons and Correctional Services.
  • Armed warders: Rathore said all watchtowers in district jails across Tamil Nadu will now have armed prison warders authorised to open fire when under attack or to prevent a jailbreak.
  • Historical context: This was the biggest prison inmate escape since 1995 when 43 LTTE militants escaped from Vellore Fort via a 153-foot tunnel at Tippu Mahal.
