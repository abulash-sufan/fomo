India rejects UN rapporteur's 'India-based' label for Dawood Ibrahim's D-Company

  • What happened: UN Special Rapporteur Ben Saul described Dawood Ibrahim-led D-Company as "India-based" in a counter-terrorism report. India rejected this label at the UN Third Committee on October 21, 2026.
  • Key details: India's First Secretary Raghoo Puri said the report contained "glaring inaccuracies." He clarified that Dawood Ibrahim has been wanted in India since 1993 and was listed under the UN 1267 sanctions regime for Al-Qaeda links while operating from outside India.
  • Reaction: Puri stated that such incompetence by special rapporteurs causes "tremendous harm" to the UN. He emphasized that Dawood resides in the same country where Osama bin Laden was found and killed in Abbottabad in 2011.
  • What's next: India called for high standards of impartiality from UN special rapporteurs. The diplomatic row highlights ongoing tensions over factual accuracy in UN reports related to counter-terrorism.

Tamil Nadu

Schools shut in five TN districts as heavy rain pounds state

  • What happened: District collectors declared a holiday for all schools in Tirunelveli, Tenkasi, Thoothukudi, Kanniyakumari and Tirupattur on Thursday following overnight moderate to heavy rainfall.
  • Rainfall data: Mamallapuram recorded the highest rainfall at 9 cm, while Tenkasi and Sattankulam received 6 cm each; Tirunelveli, Mayiladuthurai and Tiruvarur logged 5 cm each according to the Regional Meteorological Centre.
  • Chennai impact: Chennai's MGR Nagar recorded 15 cm of rain, with several neighbourhoods receiving 10-15 cm, prompting a yellow alert for the city though no school closure was ordered for Thursday.
  • Forecast: IMD predicts heavy to very heavy rain at isolated places with thunderstorms and gusty winds of 40-50 kmph on October 22 across 18 districts including Coimbatore, Nilgiris and Madurai, continuing on October 23 over Kanniyakumari, Nilgiris and Coimbatore.
  • Weather system: A low-pressure area over the southwest Bay of Bengal off the Tamil Nadu coast is driving the wet spell, which is expected to continue until October 27.
  • Puducherry closure: All government, government-aided and private schools in Puducherry and Karaikal were also declared closed on Thursday by Education Minister A Namachivayam.
  • Fishermen advisory: Mechanised boat fishermen in Thoothukudi have been advised not to venture into the sea due to continuous heavy rainfall.
