Ratan Sheikh killed in Bengal police encounter; fifth since BJP rule

  • The encounter: Ratan Sheikh, accused of shooting a police constable in Santiniketan on November 13, was killed in a gunfight with police on Sunday at a brick kiln in Nanoor, Birbhum.
  • Background: Sheikh, a history-sheeter absconding after allegedly injuring constable Biswajit Das in the leg, was traced during a manhunt; Hindustan Times says he faced 36 criminal cases.
  • Details: Police said Sheikh led them to a pistol near a bridge in Nanoor, then suddenly opened fire, hitting a police vehicle; officers returned fire in self-defence.
  • Reaction: Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari asked reporters, "If someone opens fire at the police, should the police offer them sweets," asserting criminals have no place in the State.
  • Numbers: This was the fifth police encounter in West Bengal in six months since the BJP came to power; of the four earlier men killed, three were accused in rape and murder of minor girls.
  • What's next: The Birbhum district police had made no official statement on Sheikh's death till 7 pm on Sunday.
