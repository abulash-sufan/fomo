India rejects Pannun US lawsuit against NSA Doval

  • What happened: India rejected a US civil lawsuit filed by Khalistani separatist Gurpatwant Singh Pannun against NSA Ajit Doval and former RAW chief Samant Goel, calling it a 'fabricated and malicious attack' with no legal merit.
  • Key details: A US federal court allowed Pannun to serve summons on Doval and Goel through Indian judicial channels, while the US State Department transmitted the suit to the Indian Embassy under the Foreign Sovereign Immunities Act, stating it took no position on the merits.
  • Reaction: MEA spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal dismissed the case as frivolous, citing Doval's diplomatic immunity and insisting judicial documents must be routed through the Ministry of Law and Justice under the Hague Service Convention, not via the Embassy.
  • Background: The lawsuit alleges Indian officials conspired to assassinate Pannun in New York in 2023; Indian businessman Nikhil Gupta pleaded guilty in the related criminal case, while Indian authorities arrested intelligence official Vikash Yadav separately.
  • What's next: US District Judge Katherine Polk Failla directed Pannun to prepare service documents by November 30, including details of Indian legal procedure and records of previous Hague Convention service attempts.

Delhi launches Namo Yamuna River Safari at Sonia Vihar

  • What happened: Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta launched the 'Namo Yamuna River Safari', the capital's first recreational cruise service, operating between Sonia Vihar and a temple complex in Jagatpur on the river's northern stretch.
  • Key details: The electric vessel carries 30 passengers for a 35-45 minute round trip; tickets cost ₹500 with a ₹50 entry fee, and riverfront amenities include food kiosks, a restaurant, and a children's zone.
  • Reaction: CM Gupta linked the project to PM Modi's leadership, while Tourism Minister Kapil Mishra said such initiatives were previously unimaginable and announced plans for a Krishna-themed light-and-sound show and more boats.
  • Background: The service is developed by the Inland Waterways Authority of India under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, in collaboration with DDA, DJB, and Delhi Tourism, utilizing National Waterway-110 designated under the 2016 Act.
  • Impact: Water Minister Parvesh Sahib Singh said contracts for upgrading sewage treatment plants have been awarded to improve water quality, comparing the project to the Sabarmati and Varanasi riverfront developments.
