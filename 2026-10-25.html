India

PM Modi inaugurates ₹169-crore RBI data centre, cybersecurity institute in Odisha

  • What happened: Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the Reserve Bank of India's ₹169-crore data centre and Cybersecurity Training Institute in Bhubaneswar, Odisha on Sunday. The 18.55-acre facility at Info Valley-II in Jatni was built with its foundation stone laid by then RBI governor Shaktikanta Das on March 22, 2023.
  • Key details: The data centre will host mission-critical payment and settlement systems, forming a secure digital foundation for India's technology-driven financial economy. The Cybersecurity Training Institute will focus on capability building, research, and sector-wide training. The location was chosen for geographical stability, away from seismic zones and international borders.
  • Reactions: PM Modi emphasized that wars are now fought economically and stressed protecting India's banking system from cyber terrorists. Odisha CM Mohan Charan Majhi said the facility will strengthen digital infrastructure and give new momentum to financial data security and digital economy growth.
  • What's next: The facilities are designed to provide a secure, resilient, scalable computing ecosystem for the RBI and wider financial sector. Senior leaders including Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman, RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra, and Odisha Governor Brajmohan Ram attended the event.

  • IAF Air Show

    Prime Minister Narendra Modi attended the grand finale of the Indian Air Force's 94th anniversary aerial display at Varanasi's Namo Ghat on Sunday. The three-day 'Wings over Ganges' event featured frontline fighters including SU-30MKI, Rafale, and Tejas, along with transport aircraft and helicopter display teams. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath accompanied PM Modi. Before the air show, Modi inaugurated a garment manufacturing facility for Arvind Limited in Mirzamurad. The event included tri-service participation with Army and Navy aircraft alongside IAF's Surya Kiran and Sarang teams. Approximately 11,900 security personnel were deployed across 32 zones, 50 sectors, and 80 sub-sectors. CM Yogi Adityanath directed officials to avoid unnecessary barricading to ensure easy access to Ganga ghats while maintaining strict security. The event was themed 'Wings over Ganges' and paused on Friday due to state legislative council elections. PM Modi arrived at Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport around 10 am and reached Namo Ghat by noon to witness the aerial display as chief guest.

  • EU FTA Talks

    India and the European Union are working to finalise the Free Trade Agreement before December 17-18 to coincide with PM Modi's expected Brussels visit. The European Commission proposed a fast-track procedure to expedite approval, using the original English version instead of waiting for translations into 24 languages. Commerce Secretary Rajesh Agrawal is expected in Brussels this week to resolve procedural issues. Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal is visiting Germany and the Netherlands between October 22 and 27. The FTA was announced on January 27 and both sides want to sign it during Modi's return from the G20 meeting in Miami. Goyal emphasized the transformative potential of the India-EU FTA in expanding market access, strengthening supply chains, and creating new avenues for trade and investment during his meetings in Germany. After the European Council approves the FTA, India and the EU can sign the agreement. All timelines remain fluid until officially announced, but things are moving at a fast pace from both ends.

    Sources (1)Hindustan Times
  • Indo-Australia Drill

    Indian and Australian army troops are conducting joint combat drills at the Foreign Training Node in Thiruvananthapuram as part of Exercise AUSTRAHIND 2026. The 14-day exercise runs from October 19 to November 1 and features specialized activities including combat swimming, ambush strategies, and drone manoeuvring. Approximately 240 personnel are participating with 120 soldiers each from the Indian Army's Garhwal Rifles and Australia's 13th Infantry Brigade. For the first time, an observer delegation from New Zealand is also participating. The exercise focuses on joint sub-conventional operations in island and coastal environments. Training activities span jungle, island, and coastal terrain, aiming to strengthen operational coordination and combat readiness between the two armies. The theme aligns with Chapter VII of the UN Mandate on peacekeeping operations. This is the fifth edition of Exercise AUSTRAHIND, designed to boost operational synergy between the Indian and Australian armies through specialized combat training in diverse terrains.

Saudi Arabia intercepts Houthi missiles, reopens Riyadh airport

  • What happened: The Saudi-led coalition intercepted a ballistic missile, a drone, and a cruise missile targeting Riyadh and Khamis Mushait on Sunday morning, followed by 10 ballistic missiles aimed at southern Saudi Arabia.
  • Casualties and damage: Falling debris from intercepted missiles in Khamis Mushait and Ahad Rafidah injured two people, damaged three residential buildings, and seven vehicles, according to Saudi Civil Defence.
  • Houthi claims: Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree claimed attacks on three Saudi airports, energy installations, a pipeline, and an airbase, warning Riyadh airport remains a military target.
  • Holy sites dispute: Saudi Civil Defence reported intercepting missiles over Mecca and Medina regions on Saturday; the Houthis denied targeting the holy cities, calling the claim a 'huge lie'.
  • Airport reopening: King Khalid International Airport resumed operations on Sunday with Terminal 2 for international flights and Terminal 1 for domestic, after a Houthi attack on October 10 killed 13 and wounded over 300.
  • Yemen ground fighting: Houthis advanced on Jabal Sabr mountain overlooking Taiz, besieging the city and cutting the main road to Aden, where 800,000 people are trapped according to the UN.
  • India advisory: India advised its nationals in Saudi Arabia against filming missile interceptions, warning of legal liability, and confirmed Air India flights to Riyadh and Dammam suspended until November 7.

IMD issues red alert for TN, cyclone to hit Andhra Oct 28

  • What happened: The IMD said a depression over the Bay of Bengal will intensify into a deep depression on October 25 and become a very severe cyclonic storm by October 27, crossing the Andhra Pradesh coast between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam on the morning of October 28 with winds of 115-125 kmph gusting to 140 kmph.
  • Key details: A red alert for extremely heavy rainfall has been issued for Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaikal; cyclone warning signals have been hoisted at nine ports including Chennai, Nagapattinam, Cuddalore, Karaikal, Pamban and Thoothukudi.
  • Rain forecast: For October 25, isolated extremely heavy rain is forecast in Theni, Dindigul and Madurai; heavy to very heavy rain is likely in 15 districts including Cuddalore, Mayiladuthurai, Nagapattinam, Thanjavur, the Nilgiris, Coimbatore and Tiruchirappalli.
  • Impact: Very heavy rain triggered mudslides in the Nilgiris on the night of October 23-24, burying a government bus and killing driver Janagaraj; over 100 students from Kerala were stranded but evacuated safely, and the Ooty-Mettupalayam highway was blocked.
  • Rainfall data: Burliar recorded 25 cm of rain, Kil Kotagiri Estate 20 cm, Coonoor PTO 17 cm, and Mettupalayam 15 cm in the 24 hours ending October 25 morning.
  • Response: Authorities shut tourist spots in the Nilgiris, suspended mountain train services, closed ghat roads between 6 pm and 7 am for three days, and advised tourists against visiting the district.
  • What's next: Heavy to very heavy rain is likely in Sivaganga, Ramanathapuram and Pudukkottai on October 26, with isolated heavy rain in Ramanathapuram on October 27; temperatures are expected to remain near normal until October 28.
