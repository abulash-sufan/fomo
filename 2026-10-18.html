Warning of pandal collapse may have triggered Kolkata Durga Puja stampede

  • What happened: A warning that a Durga Puja pandal in north Kolkata could collapse may have triggered panic and contributed to a stampede that killed 19 people and injured 25 on Saturday evening during Saptami celebrations amid heavy rain.
  • Key details: Police are examining a video that purportedly captures the announcement: 'The condition of the pandal is very bad. It will collapse. Please leave.' Investigators are looking into who made the announcement and whether it caused people to rush towards the exit.
  • Reactions: West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari blamed the alleged callousness of organisers and said the incident would be investigated. He also suggested that an announcement by the puja committee over the microphone may have triggered the rush.
  • What's next: Seven accused, including the puja committee’s secretary and vice-president and two private security employees, were sent to 10-day police custody. A suo motu case of culpable homicide not amounting to murder has been registered. Police are collecting CCTV footage, eyewitness statements, and forensic evidence.
  • Impact: The state government announced ₹25 lakh and a Group D government job for the family of each deceased. Injured people will receive medical support. The central government announced ₹2 lakh for each deceased’s next of kin and ₹50,000 for each injured person.

Heavy rains lash Tamil Nadu districts including Chennai on Oct 18

  • What happened: Heavy to very heavy rainfall occurred across Tamil Nadu districts including Chennai, Kancheepuram, Chengalpet, Salem, Krishnagiri, Tiruchi, Ariyalur, Nilgiris, Dharmapuri, Tiruvallur, Cuddalore, Tiruvannamalai, Erode and Puducherry on October 18, 2026, due to cyclonic circulation and convective cloud development.
  • Key details: Parrys recorded 36 cm of rainfall, Kathivakkam 34.4 cm, Meenambakkam 28.5 cm, Chennai Central 27.4 cm, Ennore 21.5 cm and Tiruvottiyur 22.4 cm between 6 p.m. Oct 17 and 6 a.m. Oct 18, 2026, as per Greater Chennai Corporation's Integrated Command and Control Centre.
  • Reactions: TNSDMA sent mobile alerts urging caution; Tamil Nadu government directed ministers and officials for relief work; nearly 15,000 conservancy staff, 650 pumps, NDRF and four SDRF teams were deployed; nine relief centres set up; subway traffic restored after water removal.
  • What's next: IMD forecast heavy rain at isolated places with thunderstorms over Chennai, Chengalpattu, Villuppuram, Tiruchirappalli, Perambalur, Tiruvannamalai, Kancheepuram, Erode, Nilgiris, Coimbatore, Krishnagiri, Salem, Tiruvallur, Kallakurichi, Cuddalore, Ariyalur districts; moderate rain likely elsewhere in TN and Karaikal.
