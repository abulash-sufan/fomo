Govt calls four-day Parliament session amid delimitation buzz

  • What happened: The Union government announced a four-day special sitting of Parliament from November 30 to December 3, 2026, to transact essential government business, subject to exigencies.
  • Key details: Lok Sabha to reconvene on November 30 and Rajya Sabha on December 2, with proceedings possibly concluding on December 3. The session aims to push through the Delimitation Bill and the Constitutional Amendment Bill for 33 per cent women's reservation from 2029.
  • Reactions: Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju requested Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla and Rajya Sabha Chairman CP Radhakrishnan to reconvene the Houses. The Congress will hold a meeting of its Parliamentary Strategy Group on Friday to discuss strategy.
  • What's next: The government must give three days' notice as per parliamentary rules. The session comes amid ongoing debate over delimitation, women's reservation, and the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls.
