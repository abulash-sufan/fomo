  • Krishna water talks

    Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay is scheduled to meet Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu in Vijayawada on Tuesday to discuss the supply of Krishna river water to Chennai under the Telugu Ganga Project. Under the 1983 agreement, 15 TMC of Krishna water is to be delivered annually at the Tamil Nadu border for Chennai’s drinking water needs, but low storage in Srisailam, Somasila and Kandaleru reservoirs complicates full supply. Sources say AP government cannot release the full quantity but may consider a proportionate release from available storage in Kandaleru and Somasila, with a partial release of up to 5 TMC as a possible compromise. The meeting recalls the January 12, 2017 meeting when then CM O. Panneerselvam sought Krishna water for Chennai, and Naidu agreed in principle to a partial release of around 2.5 TMC based on availability.
