India

Cabinet approves ₹2,009 crore bonus for 1.14 mn railway staff

  • What happened: The Union Cabinet approved a productivity-linked bonus of ₹2,009.61 crore for 1,140,770 non-gazetted railway employees on Wednesday, equivalent to 78 days' wages.
  • Key details: Each eligible employee will receive a maximum payout of ₹17,951, based on a wage ceiling of ₹7,000 and 78 days' calculation, unchanged since 2014-15.
  • Reactions: Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw called the bonus a Deepawali gift, while the Indian Railways Technical Supervisors' Association urged raising the wage ceiling to ₹18,000 plus DA for higher pay levels.
  • What's next: The bonus aims to incentivise performance following record freight loading of 1,670 million tonnes and passenger traffic of nearly 7.41 billion in 2025-26.

  • Maharashtra drought

    Over 85% of Maharashtra's talukas, or 305 out of 358, have been declared drought-hit after adding 40 more regions, Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis said on Wednesday. The state will deposit ₹5,000 in farmers' bank accounts before Diwali as initial financial assistance, as full administrative processes cannot conclude before the festival. All 12 standard state drought relief concessions are extended to these talukas, and outdated 1992 restrictions on educational fee waivers for farmers' children have been removed.
